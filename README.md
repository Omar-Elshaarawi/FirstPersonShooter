Game Description:
The name of the game is Berserk, and it is a first-person shooter game. The game is a maze, and the player must find their way into the boss chamber in order to defeat the golem. 
Once the golem is defeated, the player then wins the game. The player has ten health points. Once they die, they can either restart the game from the beginning or quit the game. The player has a gun that can shoot five times before reloading.
The game also includes four different enemies. The first enemy is the slow zombie that does melee damage of one heart per hit; the second enemy is the running zombie that moves much faster and also does one damage per melee attack.
The third enemy is the cannon knights that shoot a fireball that does one damage per hit, and lastly, the boss is a golem with five hearts of health, and he also shoots fireballs from cannons on his shoulders that do one damage per hit.
